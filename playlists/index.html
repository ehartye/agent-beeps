    How these were made

    Every track is a plain JSON score (chords, patterns, sections, mix moves) played by synthesized instruments and rendered offline in a browser. Nothing is sampled or recorded. Open a track's score link to read exactly what plays, bar by bar. Each set is levelled to its own loudness target so a dinner party, a study session and a workout each sit at a sensible volume.

    The tempo and level choices follow what could be found about each situation, and the evidence is uneven; each set's notes say which parts are well supported and which are convention.
