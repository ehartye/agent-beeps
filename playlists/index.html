    How these were made

    Scores, not recordings.

    Every track is a plain JSON score (chords, patterns, sections, mix moves) played by synthesized instruments and rendered offline in a browser. Nothing is sampled. Open a track's score link to read exactly what plays, bar by bar.

    Levelled per set.

    Each set is rendered to its own loudness target so a dinner party, a study session and a workout each sit at a sensible volume. The tempo and level choices follow what could be found about each situation; the evidence is uneven, and each set's notes say which parts are well supported and which are convention.
